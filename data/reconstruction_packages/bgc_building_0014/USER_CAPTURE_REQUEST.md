# Central Square user capture request

New capture is **required to close fidelity gaps**, but it is not a blocker for the M7 LOD1 reconstruction.

Capture in diffuse daylight where practical. Keep original files and EXIF. Do not crop, perspective-correct, or apply AI enhancement. Record capture date and stand location for every shot.

## Priority 1 — public entrances

- **Shot A — 5th Avenue overview:** stand on the west side of 5th Avenue opposite Central Square and face east. Frame the complete west facade from pavement to parapet with both building corners visible. Purpose: locate the public approach within the facade.
- **Shot B — 5th Avenue portal:** stand square to the visible west-side public portal and face approximately east. Show the complete portal, canopy/recess, full height, and one structural/storefront bay on each side. Purpose: resolve portal width, depth, columns, and hierarchy.
- **Shot C — west portal obliques:** take one view from 10–20 m north of Shot B facing southeast and one from 10–20 m south facing northeast. Purpose: resolve recess depth and corner relationships.
- **Shot D — High Street overview:** stand in the BHS Central pedestrian corridor south of the building and face north. Frame the whole ground interface and parapet, with southwest and southeast ends visible where vegetation permits. Purpose: locate south-side entrances and setbacks.
- **Shot E — every other public portal:** photograph each suspected south/north entrance facade-normal, full height, with the complete portal and both adjacent structural bays visible. Include a storefront or corner landmark so it can be assigned unambiguously.

## Priority 2 — east/service side and vehicular access

- **Shot F — northeast junction:** from the publicly accessible 30th Street end of the east-side passage, face southwest and include the north/east corner plus the full passage width.
- **Shot G — east elevation strip:** walk the accessible passage north-to-south and take facade-normal frames facing west with about 30% overlap. Record every loading door, ramp, service opening, screen, setback, or blank-wall transition that is safely visible.
- **Shot H — southeast junction:** from the High Street end, face northwest and include the south/east corner. Purpose: close facade continuity and locate any vehicular/service access.

## Priority 3 — west and south continuity

- Take overlapping, facade-normal frames along 5th Avenue and the BHS Central pedestrian side.
- Include upper parapet, ground storefront line, structural/recess rhythm, and both corners; avoid relying on tenant advertising as architectural evidence.

## Optional — roof

- If a lawful elevated public viewpoint is available, take recent oblique roof images showing parapets, screens, and mechanical plant. Do not use restricted areas or drones without permission.

Minimum useful delivery: 12–18 entrance/perimeter images plus a simple note or phone-map screenshot marking camera positions and viewing directions.
