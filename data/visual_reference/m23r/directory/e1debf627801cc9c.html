App for international tourist

An official application for foreign customers visiting Japan that makes shopping more convenient and enjoyable at Isetan Mitsukoshi Group department stores in Japan

  • *Eligibility: foreign passport holders (everyone who comes to Japan as a non-resident/international tourist and stays for less than 6 months).
  • *Some goods and services are ineligible for discount.
  • *A fee will be charged for tax free shopping precessing.
